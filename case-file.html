Operations playbook (05_Operations_Playbook_Giveaway_Rules.docx)

ApprovalsA new or changed giveaway requires ALL THREE: signed sponsor contract/amendment, written VP Marketing approval, and Ballpark Operations capacity sign-off. Conditional approvals ('pending X') are not approvals until X is met.
Lock dateNo giveaway may be added after the promo lock date (June 4 for Rally Weekend) without a written exception from the VP Marketing and the Ops Manager.
Delivery cutoffAll giveaway pallets must be at their gates at least 48 hours before gates open. Late deliveries are an exception.
Units of measureDeliveries must be recorded in EA (each). If a pallet is scanned in CS (cases), convert using the Item Master case pack before reconciling.
Damaged goodsDamaged units are NOT distributable. Distributable Qty = Qty - Damaged Qty.
Reconciliation tolerancePer gate, distributable quantity within +/-1% of allocation = MATCH. Outside 1% = EXCEPTION requiring an owner and a fix.
DuplicatesDock scanners can create a second row when a pallet is re-scanned. A repeated Delivery ID is a duplicate, even if other fields differ slightly.
Vendor servicesFireworks and entertainment are vendor services - no warehouse delivery is expected.
Staffing1 giveaway staffer per 1,250 items per gate, minimum 2 per gate. A gate distributing two items needs +3 staff.
Dock capacityEach gate has 4 pallet bays. Items for cancelled or future promotions must stay in the Central Warehouse.
Case packsMAG-SCHED27 = 250/cs | TWL-RR27 = 100/cs | BBH-RETRO27 = 24/cs | BAT-MINI27 = 50/cs | HAT-BKT27 = 40/cs
Scorebook abbreviations

Glossary

The front-office shorthand used throughout the labs.

Make-goodThe credit or replacement a sponsor receives when we deliver less than the contract guarantees.
UOM · EA · CSUnit of measure. EA = each (one unit); CS = case. Convert cases to units with the Item Master case pack.
Case packUnits per case, e.g. 24 bobbleheads per case, 100 towels per case.
POPurchase order. No PO means nothing has been ordered from the vendor.
STMSeason Ticket Member. STM-only items need their own scan lane.
RedlineA marked-up counter-proposal to a contract. Not an agreement until both sides sign.
CountersignedSigned by both parties. Only then is an amendment in force.
Lock dateThe date after which no new promotional items may be added (June 4 for Rally Weekend).
DistributableUnits that can actually go to fans: delivered quantity minus damaged units, on time for the game.
Net contributionAttendance × (margin − variable cost) + sponsorship − promotional costs − fixed costs.
Per capRevenue per fan through the gates (e.g., merchandise per cap).
ToleranceThe allowed difference between plan and actual (±1% per gate) before it counts as an exception.
