    What it takes

    The math, in full Check it yourself

    A = Fri + Sat + Sun
    Net = A × (Margin − Variable) + Sponsorship − Promo − Fixed × 3
    Break-even A = ⌈(Promo + Fixed × 3 − Sponsorship) ÷ (Margin − Variable)⌉
    Objective A = ⌈(Objective + Promo + Fixed × 3 − Sponsorship) ÷ (Margin − Variable)⌉

    Not modeled: weather refunds, sponsor relationship value, concessions lift on hot days, and the cost of a Riverbend replacement night. A prototype for discussion. Validate against Rally_Weekend_Budget_Model.xlsx before you rely on it.
