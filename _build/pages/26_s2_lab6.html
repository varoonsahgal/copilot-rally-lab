The mission

The game ends. The season doesn't.

Rally Weekend will come and go, but Friday's make-good report, next month's promotions and the July 13 magnet night won't wait. Status chasing is where good teams lose hours every week.

Set up a scheduled prompt that delivers a weekly status and outstanding-commitments digest automatically. Then pick the one workflow you will improve on Monday.

Game plan

  • Write a recurring status and outstanding-commitments prompt.
  • Schedule it (or preview how, subject to your organization's settings).
  • Tell scheduled analysis apart from automated business actions.
  • Choose the right Copilot tool for a workflow of your own.

Play-by-play

  1. Write the digest prompt and run it once

    Run it manually first. Never schedule a prompt you haven't seen work.

    Create my weekly Promotions status digest for the past 7 days. Look across my email, Teams chats and meetings, and files I've worked on that mention promotions, giveaways, sponsors or [[project name, e.g. Rally Weekend]]. Sections: 1. Outstanding commitments: who promised what to whom, the due date, and status (Open / Overdue / Done), with a link to the source. 2. Approvals still pending: item, which approval is missing, owner. 3. Important changes since last week: numbers, dates or scope that changed, shown as old -> new, with the source. 4. Sponsor follow-ups due in the next 7 days. 5. Top 3 things I should do Monday morning. Only include items with a source. If a section has nothing, write "Nothing found" rather than guessing.
    Classroom note

    Your mailbox doesn't contain the fictional Rally Weekend emails, so run this against your real work (swap in a real project name), or point it at the lab files: "...using the files in my Rally Weekend Task Force folder".

    If you saved the lab files to OneDrive earlier, Copilot may find them on its own. In our live test it filled all five sections, with citations, from the budget model, the allocation and warehouse workbooks and a decision brief saved in Inning 5. It also repeated a figure from that draft brief. Check the source behind every figure before you trust the digest.

  2. Schedule it

    • After the prompt runs, open the … (more options) menu under Copilot's response and choose Schedule this prompt. Tested live in M365 Copilot · Nov 2026
    • In the Create a schedule dialog, check the prefilled Prompt, then set Starts (a date, and a time in 30-minute steps) and Every: Day, Week or Month. For a weekly digest choose Week, select M (and deselect any other day already selected), and pick 07:30 AM.
    • Until defaults to about two weeks out. Change it, or use the trash icon to remove the end date. Receive an email when responses are ready is checked by default. Click Save, or close the dialog with X to back out (there's no Cancel button).
    • Results arrive in Copilot Chat, often with a notification, on each run.
    Rain delay · availability

    Scheduled prompts depend on license, release and admin settings, and may limit how many you can create or how often they run. Not available? Save the prompt to your prompt gallery or a pinned note, and put a 5-minute weekly calendar reminder on it. Same habit, one extra click.

  3. Know what a scheduled prompt does, and doesn't do

    Scheduled prompt doesScheduled prompt does not
    Read and summarize what you already have access toUpdate the ticketing, inventory or contract system
    Flag overdue commitments and pending approvalsSend emails to sponsors or fans on your behalf
    Compare this week to last weekApprove, reject or reorder anything
    Deliver results to you to reviewGuarantee it found everything (check the sources)

    Need the system to act, like creating a task or notifying a channel? That's an automated workflow (for example in Power Automate or Copilot Studio), with an owner, testing and governance.

  4. Two more recurring prompts worth stealing

    Every weekday at 8:00 AM during event week: summarize anything new about [[event name]] since yesterday (emails, chats, meetings, file changes). Flag anything that changes a quantity, date, approval or sponsor commitment. Keep it under 150 words. Every Friday at 3:00 PM: list the meetings I attended this week where I was assigned an action item, with the action, due date and whether I've replied or completed it.
